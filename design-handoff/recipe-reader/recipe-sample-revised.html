Golden Spiced Oat + Almond Breakfast Cake

November 12, 2026♡

There’s a particular grey that settles over late autumn mornings — the light thin, the air sharp, the body asking to slow down. This is the cake I make when I want breakfast to feel like a small act of grounding rather than a rush out the door.

Warm oats, toasted almond, and a generous hand of cardamom and cinnamon make it deeply vata- and kapha-friendly: nourishing, a little sweet, never heavy. It keeps for three days and only gets better with a smear of ghee.

The Recipe
Golden Spiced Oat + Almond Breakfast Cake

Recipe Notes

Make it your own: swap the almonds for toasted walnuts in deep winter, and fold in a grated apple if you want extra moisture. Gluten-free oats work perfectly.

Flavor Notes

Warming and grounding, with a gentle sweetness that settles rather than spikes.

Ingredients

For the cake
2 cupsrolled oats
1 cupalmond flour
2 tspground cardamom
1 tspcinnamon
½ tspground ginger
¼ tspfine sea salt
2eggs (or 2 flax eggs)
⅓ cupmaple syrup
¼ cupmelted ghee, plus more to serve
¾ cupwarm milk of choice
For the topping
⅓ cupsliced almonds
1 tbspmaple syrup
—flaky salt, to finish

Instructions

1

Prep

Heat the oven to 350°F and line an 8-inch square pan. Warm the milk and ghee together until just loose.

2

Mix

Whisk the oats, almond flour, spices, and salt. In a second bowl beat the eggs, maple, ghee, and milk, then fold the wet into the dry until just combined.

3

Top + bake

Spread into the pan, scatter the almonds, drizzle with maple, and finish with flaky salt. Bake 28–32 minutes until the center springs back.

4

Serve

Cool 10 minutes, cut into squares, and serve warm with an extra smear of ghee.
